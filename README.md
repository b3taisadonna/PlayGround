## 8 Ball Pool

A fan-made, Miniclip-inspired 8-ball billiards game. Vanilla HTML, CSS, Canvas and ES modules — no build step, no external assets, no network calls.

### Play

- **VS CPU** — Casual (100), Pro (500) and Legend (2,000) coin tables. The entry fee is deducted up front and a win pays double.
- **2 Players** — pass-and-play on one device.
- **Aim & shoot** — drag backwards from the cue ball like pulling back a real cue. The further you pull, the harder the shot. Release to strike.
- **Cue spin** — use the cue-ball pad for follow, draw or side spin; **RESET** centers the strike point.
- **Controls** — tap/click the table to aim and shoot, `Esc` or the menu button to pause, `M` to mute, and the fullscreen button for a distraction-free table.

The layout adapts to phones and tablets in portrait and landscape, with touch-friendly controls and safe-area padding.

### Rules

Open table after the break; the first legally pocketed ball assigns solids or stripes. Hit your own group first, then pot a ball or drive a ball to a cushion. Fouls — scratch, no contact, wrong group first, no rail after contact or an illegal break — give the opponent ball in hand. Clear your group before pocketing the 8; potting it early or scratching on it loses the frame. An 8 on the break is re-racked.

### Run locally

The repository root is the site root, so any static server works:

```bash
PORT=3000 bash start.sh
# or
python3 -m http.server 3000
```

Then open <http://localhost:3000>.
